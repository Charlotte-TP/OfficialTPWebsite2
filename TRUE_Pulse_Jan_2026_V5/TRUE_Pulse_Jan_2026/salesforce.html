Bring Phone Calls Into Your Salesforce CRM

Phone calls are often the most valuable leads — but without CRM integration, they remain disconnected from your sales pipeline.

TRUE Pulse automatically pushes call data into Salesforce, ensuring every conversation is captured, attributed, and acted upon.

  • Automatically create or update Leads and Contacts
  • Log calls as Activities against Salesforce records
  • Capture marketing attribution alongside sales data
  • Improve follow-ups, conversions, and deal visibility

How the Salesforce Integration Works

  1. Authorize TRUE Pulse to connect with your Salesforce account
  2. Define how calls should map to Leads, Contacts, or Accounts
  3. Configure field mappings and call activity rules
  4. Automatically sync call data into Salesforce in real time

Flexible Salesforce Field Mapping

Every Salesforce setup is unique. TRUE Pulse adapts to your CRM — not the other way around.

Leads & Contacts

Create new leads from first-time callers or match calls to existing contacts automatically.

Activities & Call Logs

Log calls as Activities with call duration, status, source, and outcome.

Custom Fields

Map TRUE Pulse call attributes to your custom Salesforce fields for deeper reporting.

Automation Ready

Trigger Salesforce workflows, alerts, and assignments based on call events.
