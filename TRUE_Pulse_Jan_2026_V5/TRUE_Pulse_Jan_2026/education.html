Why Phone Calls Matter in the Education Sector

Admissions, counseling, eligibility checks, fee discussions, and career guidance are rarely completed through forms alone. Phone calls indicate serious intent — but without call tracking, these leads are invisible.

  • Admission and enrollment calls not attributed to marketing campaigns
  • Unclear ROI across Google Ads, social media, portals, and offline promotions
  • No insight into inquiry quality or counseling effectiveness
  • Multiple campuses, courses, and programs with fragmented lead data

How TRUE Pulse Helps Education Businesses

How Call Tracking Works for Education

  1. Assign tracking numbers to ads, landing pages, courses, and campuses
  2. Capture inbound calls with source, campaign, and program data
  3. Analyze call quality, counseling outcomes, and conversions
  4. Optimize marketing spend and boost enrollments
