Primary Text (main body text)

Dark neutral, not pure black — easier on the eyes.

Charcoal / Dark Gray: #1F2933 or #222222
✔ Best for paragraphs, long reads
✔ Works with any blue

🧭 Headings & Important Labels

Go slightly bolder to create hierarchy.

Near-black: #0F172A

Deep Navy: #0A2540
✔ Looks intentional next to blue buttons
✔ Feels premium and modern

🔗 Links & Interactive Text

Match or complement your button blue.

Standard Blue: #2563EB

Slightly muted blue: #1D4ED8

Hover state: #1E40AF

💡 Tip: Underline on hover instead of always — cleaner look.

⚠️ Accent / Highlight Text

For “attention” moments that aren’t buttons.

Teal: #0D9488 (great with blue)

Amber: #F59E0B (warnings, emphasis)

Rose / Red: #DC2626 (errors only — use sparingly)

🧩 Secondary / Helper Text

For placeholders, captions, metadata.

Cool gray: #6B7280

Lighter gray: #9CA3AF

⚠️ Don’t go lighter than this or accessibility suffers.

✅ Accessibility Quick Check

Aim for:

Body text: Contrast ratio ≥ 4.5:1

Headings: ≥ 3:1
Tools like WebAIM Contrast Checker help a ton.

🎨 Example Combo (clean & popular)

Body text: #1F2933

Headings: #0A2540

Links: #2563EB

Buttons: #2563EB (white text)

Muted text: #6B7280
