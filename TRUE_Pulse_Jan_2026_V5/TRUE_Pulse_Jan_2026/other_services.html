Call Tracking Beyond Traditional Industries

Many businesses rely on phone calls as a critical lead channel — even if they don’t fall under common verticals like real estate, healthcare, or travel.

Why Call Tracking Matters for These Businesses

  • High-value leads often prefer phone conversations
  • Sales cycles involve multiple touchpoints and follow-ups
  • Inbound calls indicate strong purchase intent
  • Marketing ROI is difficult to measure without call attribution
  • Missed calls often mean lost revenue opportunities

How TRUE Pulse Helps

Marketing Attribution
