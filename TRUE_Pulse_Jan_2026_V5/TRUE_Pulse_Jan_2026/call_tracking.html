How Dynamic Call Tracking & Attribution Works

TRUE Pulse tracks, attributes, and activates every phone call— from the first ad click to remarketing, CRM sync, and revenue reporting.

1

Someone finds your business online

A potential customer visits your website or landing page after interacting with any marketing activity, including but not limited to organic search results, paid search ads, display ads, video, banners, email campaigns, social media, or any other online or offline promotional effort.

2

Dynamic Phone Number Displayed

TRUE Pulse uses Dynamic Number Insertion (DNI) to display a unique phone number for each website visitor. The number remains consistent throughout the visitor’s session, enabling accurate call attribution.

3

Visitor Calls the Dynamic Number

Visitors can call the displayed number from any device. Every call is accurately tracked, attributed, and intelligently routed based on your rules, with options like welcome messages, IVR, time-of-day routing, and more.

4

Call Is Tracked & Attributed

Each call is instantly attributed to the exact campaign, keyword, ad, device, and location that generated it. The tracking configuration is fully customizable—ranging from high-level source or campaign tracking to keyword-level attribution.

5

See Call Data On the TRUE Pulse Dashboard

View caller info, call recordings, duration, traffic source, campaign details, device, browser, and full UTM data instantly..

6

Call Event Is Sent to Google Analytics

TRUE Pulse pushes call events into Google Analytics (GA4), including source, campaign, call duration, and call answer status.

7

GA Events Are Imported into Google Ads

Import call events from Google Analytics into Google Ads for conversion tracking, smart bidding, and remarketing.

8

Call Data Syncs to CRM & Other Platforms

Simultaneously send call data to CRMs or tools like Zapier, enabling workflows across sales, marketing, and support platforms.
