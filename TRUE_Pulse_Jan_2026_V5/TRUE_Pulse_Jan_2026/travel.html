Why Phone Calls Drive Revenue in Travel & Hospitality

Whether it’s a hotel booking, table reservation, tour inquiry, or transport request, phone calls indicate strong intent. Without call tracking, these high-value interactions are often missing from marketing reports.

  • Bookings and reservations not attributed to ads or listings
  • Unclear ROI across OTAs, Google Ads, Maps, and social media
  • No visibility into inquiry quality or booking readiness
  • Multiple locations, properties, or services with fragmented data

How TRUE Pulse Helps Travel & Hospitality Businesses

How Call Tracking Works for Travel & Hospitality

  1. Assign tracking numbers to ads, listings, properties, and services
  2. Capture inbound calls with source, campaign, and location data
  3. Analyze booking intent, call quality, and outcomes
  4. Optimize spend to increase reservations and revenue
