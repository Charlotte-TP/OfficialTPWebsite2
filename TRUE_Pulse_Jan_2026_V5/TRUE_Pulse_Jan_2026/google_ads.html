Turn Phone Calls Into Google Ads Conversions

Many of your highest-value leads come from phone calls — but without proper tracking, Google Ads only sees clicks, not real outcomes.

TRUE Pulse bridges that gap by sending call conversion data directly into Google Ads, so you can measure and optimize what truly matters.

  • Track inbound calls as Google Ads conversions
  • Attribute calls to keywords, ads, campaigns, and extensions
  • Optimize Smart Bidding based on real call outcomes
  • Measure true ROI from call-focused campaigns

How the Google Ads Integration Works

  1. Connect your Google Ads account with TRUE Pulse
  2. Assign tracking numbers to Google Ads campaigns
  3. Define which calls count as conversions (duration, status, quality)
  4. Push call conversion events directly into Google Ads

Privacy, Security & Google Ads Policy Compliance

TRUE Pulse follows Google Ads policies and industry best practices to ensure safe, compliant data sharing.

  • No personally identifiable information (PII) is sent to Google Ads.
  • Phone numbers and user-level identifiers are securely hashed before any data transmission.
  • Conversion events are sent without exposing sensitive customer data.
  • Fully aligned with Google Ads conversion tracking requirements.

You get powerful attribution and optimization — without compromising user privacy or policy compliance.
