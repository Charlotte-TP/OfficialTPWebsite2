What Is Form Tracking?

Form tracking allows you to capture and attribute website form submissions to the exact marketing source that generated the lead — including campaign, channel, keyword, ad, and landing page.

With TRUE Pulse, every form submission becomes a measurable, actionable conversion — not just a notification email.

Why Form Tracking Matters

  • Form leads are often high-intent and sales-ready
  • Email notifications alone don’t show marketing ROI
  • Multiple campaigns may influence one submission
  • Missed or delayed responses reduce conversion rates
  • Disconnected data leads to wasted ad spend

How TRUE Pulse Form Tracking Works

  1. Add a lightweight TRUE Pulse tracking script
  2. Automatically detect and track form submissions
  3. Capture marketing attribution and session data
  4. Send leads instantly to your dashboards and tools
