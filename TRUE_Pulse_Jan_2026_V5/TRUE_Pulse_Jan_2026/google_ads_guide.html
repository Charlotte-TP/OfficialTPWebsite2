Google Ads Integration Walkthrough

Step 1: Open Google Ads Integration

  • Go to Integrations in the left navigation menu.
  • Select Google Ads.

Step 2: Connect Gmail Account

  • Click Connect.
  • Choose the Gmail account that owns the Google Ads account.

Step 3: Authorize Access

  • Click Authorize to allow True Pulse to send conversions.

Step 4: Select Google Ads Account

  • You will be redirected back to True Pulse.
  • Select the Google Ads account linked to the Gmail.

Step 5: Reopen Account Selection (If Closed)

  • Click Add Google Ads Account.
  • Select Gmail → Click Next.
  • Choose an Ads account or MCC sub-account.

Step 6: Create Mapping

  • Select the Service / Business.
  • Name the mapping.
  • Choose Automatic or Manual push mode.
  • Click Save.

Important Information

  • TRUE Pulse creates a required Conversion Action in Google Ads.
  • Do not rename, delete, or modify this Conversion Action.
  • Deleting a mapping stops conversion uploads immediately.
  • Disconnecting Gmail removes all linked Google Ads mappings.
