What Is Lead Management?

Lead management is the process of capturing, tracking, assigning, and following up on leads across their entire lifecycle — from first contact to conversion.

TRUE Pulse brings calls and forms together into a single, easy-to-use lead management system built for speed and accountability.

Why Lead Management Matters

  • Leads lose value when response times are slow
  • Scattered tools cause missed follow-ups
  • No visibility into lead status hurts sales performance
  • Marketing ROI suffers without closed-loop reporting
  • Growth becomes unscalable without structure

How TRUE Pulse Lead Management Works

  1. Capture leads automatically from calls and forms
  2. Centralize all lead data in one dashboard
  3. Assign and route leads to the right teams
  4. Track follow-ups, outcomes, and conversions
