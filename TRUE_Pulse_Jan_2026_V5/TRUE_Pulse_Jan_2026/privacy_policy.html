Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2025


TRUE Pulse is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information.

1. Purpose and Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to TRUE Pulse and its subsidiaries and affiliates (“TRUE Pulse Group”). It governs all Personal Information processed by TRUE Pulse in the course of providing services, including information relating to Customers, prospective customers, and end users whose information is processed on behalf of Customers through our Call Analytics Services.

2. What Information We Collect

Customer Information

We may collect information such as name, business name, address, email address, phone number, identification and tax documentation (PAN, Aadhaar, GST), payment details, and correspondence records.

Individual Information

Collected on behalf of Customers through Call Analytics Services, including caller ID, call recordings (if enabled), IP address, device information, browser data, session identifiers, cookies, and analytics data.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data unless explicitly required and consented to by you.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

TRUE Pulse and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to analyze website performance and user behavior. Users may disable cookies via browser settings, though some features may not function correctly.

4. How We Use Personal Information

  • Provide and manage our services
  • Verify identity and manage customer accounts
  • Perform analytics, research, and quality assurance
  • Communicate regarding support and service updates
  • Send marketing communications (with consent)
  • Comply with legal obligations

5. Data Retention

Personal Information is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill its intended purpose or comply with legal and contractual obligations. Data is securely deleted or anonymized once no longer required.

6. Data Security

TRUE Pulse implements appropriate technical and organizational measures including encryption, secure transmission (HTTPS), restricted access, and regular security reviews.

7. Data Sharing, Integrations & International Transfers

We may share Personal Information with affiliates, subprocessors, service providers, professional advisors, and authorities where legally required. Appropriate safeguards are applied for any international data transfers.

8. Third-Party Integrations

8.1 Use of Integrations

Customers may integrate TRUE Pulse with third-party platforms such as analytics, advertising, CRM, and automation tools. Data is transferred only as required to deliver integration functionality.

8.2 Nature of Data Transfer

TRUE Pulse performs outbound data transfers only and does not access or manage data inside the Customer’s third-party account.

8.3 Termination Handling

Upon termination or disabling of integrations, data transfers stop immediately. Data already transferred remains subject to the third party’s retention policies.

8.4 Customer Responsibility

Customers confirm they have obtained necessary consent and understand that TRUE Pulse cannot delete data already transmitted to third-party platforms.

9. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent by contacting privacy@truepulse.co.in.

10. Marketing and Opt-Out

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.

11. Data Breach Notification

TRUE Pulse will notify affected parties and authorities as required by law and take prompt remedial action.

12. Children’s Data

Our services are not intended for individuals under 18. Any such data identified will be deleted immediately.
