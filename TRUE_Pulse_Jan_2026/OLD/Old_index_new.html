TRUE Pulse gives you everything you need to track calls, optimize marketing performance, and turn conversations into revenue.

Real-Time Reporting & Analytics

Monitor call activity as it happens and optimize campaigns instantly based on performance, lead quality, and conversion data.

Multi-Touch Attribution

Analyze the full customer journey—not just the last touch—to understand true marketing effectiveness.

Call Recording & Quality Assurance

Record and review calls for training, compliance, and quality assurance—helping teams convert more leads.

Call Routing & Interactive Voice Response (IVR)
