Salesforce Integration Guide

Follow these steps to connect TRUE Pulse with Salesforce and start sending call details.

Step 1: Log in to Salesforce

  • Ensure you are logged in to Salesforce in the same browser.
  • In TRUE Pulse, go to Integrations → Salesforce.

Step 2: Initiate the Connection

  • Click Connect.
  • You will be redirected to Salesforce authorization.

Step 3: Authorize Access

  • Click Authorize to grant required permissions.

Step 4: Return to TRUE Pulse

  • You will be redirected back to TRUE Pulse automatically.
  • A dedicated Salesforce object is created to store call details.

Step 5: Create a Mapping

  • Click + New Mapping.
  • Select Salesforce Org and Service.
  • Name your mapping.
  • Choose a push mode: Automatic, Manual, or Both.

Important Notes

  • Do not rename or modify the Salesforce object created by TRUE Pulse.
  • Deleting a mapping stops data transfer immediately.
  • Disconnecting Salesforce removes all mappings.
